## 7. Dynamic Content Architecture & File Mapping

The Next.js codebase is structured to drive all website content dynamically from clean, single-source data files:

```
data/
├── clinicInfo.js       # Central clinic configuration: Doctor bio, phones, WhatsApp, branches, stats, B/A outcomes
├── treatments.js       # Complete 15 treatments directory with procedure steps, highlights, suitability & images
└── blogs.js            # Clinical self-care articles and dermatological guides by Dr. Megha

components/sections/
├── HeroSection.jsx             # Hero slider with Ken Burns effect & appointment form
├── BrandPhilosophy.jsx         # Dr. Megha editorial quote & 3 clinical pillars
├── PinnedShowcase.jsx          # Pin-snapped horizontal scroll showcase of all 15 treatments
├── BeforeAfterSlider.jsx       # Featured carousel slider with interactive drag comparison
├── DoctorBio.jsx               # Dr. Megha Aggarwal profile, Doctor.png, credentials & dual clinics
├── SkinQuizWidget.jsx          # Interactive 3-step recommendation lead magnet
├── TestimonialsMarquee.jsx     # Google Reviews style section with 4.9 rating & verified cards
├── BlogSection.jsx             # 3 featured clinical blogs with read links
└── CTABanner.jsx               # Final consultation booking push

components/layout/
├── Navbar.jsx                  # Header navigation with treatment dropdowns, phone & booking
└── Footer.jsx                  # Rich footer with branch maps, phones, WhatsApp & clinic hours
```

### How to Update Content Dynamically
1. **Change Phone or WhatsApp Numbers**: Edit `data/clinicInfo.js` under `clinicInfo.contact`. All header, footer, booking forms, and buttons update automatically.
2. **Add or Modify a Treatment**: Edit `data/treatments.js`. The treatment page routing `/treatments/[slug]` and menu automatically update.
3. **Update Doctor Credentials or Image**: Edit `data/clinicInfo.js` under `clinicInfo.doctor.image` (currently set to `/images/Doctor.png`).
